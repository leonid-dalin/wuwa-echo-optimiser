Mathematical gear evaluation for Wuthering Waves

When to use Macro?

Use this to define your Keep vs. Discard boundaries. It calculates whether it's statistically cheaper to keep upgrading a bad Echo or to start fresh based on the subjective value of your resources.

How to read the data & define weights:

  • EXP Weight (e.g., 1): The baseline unit. Assume 1 EXP = 1 point of value.
  • Tuner Weight (e.g., 500): Tuners are the main bottleneck. If you'd trade 500 EXP for 1 Tuner, enter 500.
  • A, B, F Tiers: Your substats are grouped by quality. A = Top Tier (CRIT), B = Good (ATK%, ER), F = Dead (Flat HP, DEF).
  • Reading the Grid: A state like "AAB" means your Echo currently has 2 A-tier stats and 1 B-tier stat. The system calculates if pushing it to level 25 is mathematically worth the cost.
